TRADEFLOW LOCAL WORKFLOW SPEC
POS-FIRST INVENTORY OS

Purpose
- TradeFlow runs as a local-first POS and inventory operating system.
- The workflow keeps the business outcome of the DeepSeek model, but the entry points are faster and more practical for daily use.
- The main trigger is sales and low-stock alerts, not manual inspection.

Core Rule
- Stock moves only from confirmed events.
- Sale confirmed: stock goes down.
- Receipt confirmed: stock goes up.
- Adjustment confirmed: stock changes only with an audit trail.

                                      │
                                      ▼

                    │  User reviews and adjusts        │

9) Role Responsibilities
Staff
- Sell products.
- Receive and confirm deliveries.
- Check low-stock alerts.

Admin
- Add and edit products.
- Build budgets.
- Adjust purchase quantities and costs.
- Review stock and reports.

10) Local-First Principle
- Do not force inspection as the main flow.
- Use low-stock alerts as the restock trigger.
- Use previous cost price as the budget starting point.
- Allow purchase-time edits before placing the order.
- Confirm receipt to update stock automatically.

End State
- TradeFlow behaves like a local inventory OS:
- sell fast
- detect low stock
- plan restock
- place purchase
- receive stock
- update inventory automatically
